1. **Vision & originality**
   A secure workspace for lawyers and authorized legal professionals handling sexual-assault cases in Vietnam. Records are classified by sensitivity; access depends on role, purpose, and approval - not merely account ownership. No administrator automatically receives unrestricted access.
2. **Trust constraint insight**
   In current systems, an administrator - or an attacker who compromises an account - could potentially read, leak, or tamper with case files, which cause victims to face social stigma, retaliation, and further harm. By using our prototype, we can ensure that no single individual can access the files alone, thus turning the system into one that victims can truly trust.
3. **Value & impact**

This approach provides victims with greater privacy and security, while sparing lawyers from the burden of holding sensitive data that could be targeted for forced disclosure. For instance, a Vietnamese case involving alleged sexual abuse of a minor, an assigned lawyer requests a forensic report. Colleagues can manage routine scheduling, but the report remains restricted until required approvals are recorded. This reduces unnecessary exposure while supporting authorized casework.
**4. Why TideCloak matters**
TideCloak manages authentication, defines roles, and secures actions. Access to more confidential documents is granted only when the identity and approvals from all key-holders, verified by TideCloak. No single server or administrator holds a "master key." In contrast, standard login systems often allow an administrator to access everything.
**5. Prototype & storytelling**
The prototype demonstrates how TideCloak protects sensitive sexual-assault case information through multi-party authorization. The demo would show a victim uploading and managing case documents, then requesting access to protected information. Access remains restricted until the required lawyer and guardian approvals are confirmed. The victim can track approval progress and view the protected document once authorization is complete. Another interaction would be how administrators cannot bypass access controls to view members’ private documents.
